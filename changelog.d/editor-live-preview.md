## Changed

- The Editor's writing view uses the shared Markdown live preview. With Quiet Markdown on, headings, emphasis, links, lists, tasks, quotes, fenced code and callouts render in place, and tables are edited as a grid over their Markdown: move between cells with Tab and Enter, drag rows and columns by their grips, insert, move, delete, align and sort from the menus, and copy and paste blocks of cells. Source shows only where the selection touches it. Tab and Shift-Tab nest list items at their text column.
- A note changed from outside while it is open keeps the caret where it was instead of moving it.
