## Added

- Plus, a paid plan bought with Stripe Checkout. Account shows a Plan section with monthly and yearly upgrade buttons, when Plus renews or ends, and a Manage billing button for Stripe's customer portal. Plus starts as soon as Stripe confirms payment, keeps going for a week after a failed renewal while Stripe retries, and ends on its own after that. Billing stays off unless the server has its Stripe settings. Storage-trial emails are no longer sent to accounts that have Plus by the time the trial ends.
