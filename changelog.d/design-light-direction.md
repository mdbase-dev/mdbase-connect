## Changed

- Redesign the portal and desktop app on one lighter interface: IBM Plex Sans, a quiet sidebar without nav icons, a focused collection Overview, status glyphs, shared controls, a Ctrl/⌘ K command menu, and inline confirmation in place of native dialogs. Sharing and your person record move to App access.
- Bring the Editor closer to the same interface: a shared language menu, a tighter quick open with neutral selection, lighter row names and quieter Settings and Types titles. Shared tokens now carry IBM Plex Sans, smaller radii and 28px controls, which apps pick up when they repin `@mdbase-dev/ui`.
