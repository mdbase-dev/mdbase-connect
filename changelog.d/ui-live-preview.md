## Added

- `@mdbase-dev/ui/live-preview` adds a live preview for CodeMirror 6 Markdown editors, with `live-preview.css`. Headings, emphasis, strikethrough, inline code, links and wikilinks, lists, tasks, quotes, rules, tables, fenced code (with a language label and Copy), callouts (collapsible, filled by type) and footnote references render in place, and the document stays plain Markdown. Source shows only where a focused selection touches it. Revealed marks sit in the margin or keep their width, so the text being edited never moves, and the reveal holds still while the mouse button is down. `indentListItem` and `outdentListItem` nest and lift list items at their text column and renumber ordered lists. Home stops at an item's text before the line start. A task's checkbox takes its bullet's place and toggles with a click or Space, and later lines of an item hang under its text.
- Live preview tables are edited in place as a grid over their Markdown source, with no separate source mode. Tab, Shift-Tab and Enter move between cells and add a row at the end; Enter on an empty last row leaves the table, and Enter at the start of the first header cell opens a line above it. A typed `|` is escaped and Shift-Enter adds a `<br>`, so a row never breaks. Deleting across cells clears their text and keeps the columns. Drag a row or column by its grip to move it (Escape cancels). Clicking a grip or the context menu (Shift-F10 from the keyboard) inserts, moves, deletes, aligns and sorts rows and columns. Alt-Up and Alt-Down move rows. Dragging, Shift-clicking or Shift-arrowing from one cell into another selects a block of cells, including empty and missing ones; Mod-A selects the table, then the note. Delete empties a block, typing starts in its first cell, and copy puts it on the clipboard as Markdown and as an HTML table, so it pastes as a table in spreadsheets and documents. Right-clicking a block copies, cuts or clears it and deletes all its rows or columns, and the menus can copy the whole table. Spreadsheet, Markdown and HTML grids paste into cells and grow the table, and an HTML table pasted outside a table becomes a Markdown table. Typing a `| a | b |` header and pressing Enter starts a table. Tables in quotes and lists keep their prefix, and on narrow screens words wrap whole and wide tables scroll. The live preview now needs `@codemirror/view` 6.42.1 or later.
