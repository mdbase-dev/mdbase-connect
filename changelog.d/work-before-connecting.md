## Added

- Apps can let people start working before they sign in or connect a collection. With `createMdbase(declaration, {unsynced: true})`, a signed-out start opens the app's unsynced collection (`phase.kind === "unsynced"`, or `mdbase.unsynced.open()` from a gesture): a local-only replica saved only in this browser, configured from the declaration, used through the same connection as a synced collection and never listed in `collections`. Files cannot be added to it yet. Every snapshot carries `unsyncedWork` (`{since}` once something has been saved there), so an app can offer to connect that work.
- `mdbase.unsynced.planTransfer({map})` and `mdbase.unsynced.transfer(plan, {onProgress})` bring the unsynced collection's records into the collection the tab has open. Records keep their IDs; a taken path is renamed beside it (`todo 2.md`); types the destination lacks are reported in `plan.missingTypes`; the app's `map` may reshape or leave out records. Transfers are idempotent: running again, from any tab or after a reload, creates only what is missing. `createApplicationFixture({unsynced})` exercises the same flow in tests. A transfer that leaves nothing behind retires the unsynced collection (`result.retired`): `unsyncedWork` clears and the next open starts afresh. `mdbase.unsynced.discard()` erases it after the person confirms. `planTransfer({addTypes: true})` adds types the destination lacks from the unsynced collection's own definitions, for a new collection made from this work. A record edited here after it was brought in comes in again as a copy beside the destination's version (outcome `changed`, `plan.counts.changed`); the destination's version is never overwritten. Retiring is refused while the unsynced collection is still open in another tab or something was saved there after the plan.
