## Added

- The Editor can show two notes side by side. **Open to the side** in a note's menu, Ctrl or ⌘ with Alt and a click on a link, or the **Split right** and **Split down** commands open a second pane with its own tabs and history. Clicking a pane or pressing Alt+O moves the focus between them, the list and quick open act on the focused pane, and the split, its direction and both panes' tabs come back when the collection reopens. Closing a pane's last tab closes the pane. Every new command can be given a shortcut in Settings. Phones keep showing one note.
