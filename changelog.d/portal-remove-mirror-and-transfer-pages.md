## Removed

- The portal's approval pages for legacy mirror pairing (`/mirror/:id`) and for moving a hosted collection's main copy to a computer (`/transfer/:id`, "Move main copy"). Only the legacy connector, which was removed earlier, could start either flow. An old link to either page now opens your account. Legacy accounts can no longer approve a new synced folder or a move of the main copy in the portal; existing hosted collections and their synced folders are still shown and can still be disconnected.
