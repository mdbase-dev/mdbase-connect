## Changed

- The Editor's panes are now a panel workspace, and are no longer limited to two. **Split right** and **Split down** split the focused pane again, a tab can be dragged to another pane or to the edge of one to open a new pane there, and the line between panes drags to resize them. Alt+O moves to the next pane. The arrangement comes back when the collection reopens, and tabs saved by earlier versions are restored into it.
