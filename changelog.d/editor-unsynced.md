## Added

- Editor lets people write before they connect. Signed out, it opens a workspace saved only in this browser ("Continue without connecting" on the signed-out screen), shows "Saved only in this browser" instead of Connected, and offers "Connect to sync your work" once something is saved. With a collection open, it offers to bring that work in or, after confirming, discard it. Files cannot be attached before connecting. Editor now pins the shared UI to 0.1.0-beta.163.
