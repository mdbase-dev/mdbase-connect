## Added

- The editor shows a note's history. Open **Version history** beside a note to see its saved versions by day, with who made each one (you or a collaborator, the app or device, or an edit made outside mdbase), compare a version with the note as it is now, and restore it after confirming. The restore is saved like any other edit, so the text it replaces stays in history. The panel says how far back history goes, and explains when a collection has no history or the editor needs access to it.
